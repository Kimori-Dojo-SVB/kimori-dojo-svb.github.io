## Training Levels

### Beginners
New to aikido? Start here! No prior martial arts experience needed. Classes begin with fundamentals and progress at a comfortable pace. Most beginners train 6-12 months before testing for their first rank.

### Intermediate
Practitioners with 1-3 years of training. Focus shifts to deeper technique understanding, more complex movements, and increased physical conditioning.

### Advanced
4th+ year practitioners and higher ranks. Advanced students assist in instruction and develop teaching skills.

## What to Expect

1. **Arrival**: Arrive 10-15 minutes early on your first class
2. **Warm-up**: 10-15 minutes of stretching and conditioning
3. **Instruction**: 45-60 minutes of technique training
4. **Practice**: Partner work applying the techniques
5. **Cool-down**: Gentle stretching and bow-out

Classes are cooperative, not competitive. Everyone practices at their own level.

## Rank Advancement

Aikido advancement follows the Aikikai system:
- 6th kyu through 1st kyu (white belts)
- 1st through 3rd dan (black belts)  
- 4th+ dan (expert instructors)

Testing opportunities are available quarterly with our head instructor.

## First Class Tips

- **Arrive early** to meet the instructor and ask questions
- **Don't worry about making mistakes** - everyone does, and that's how we learn
- **Listen carefully** to instructions but don't stress about perfect execution
- **Partner with different people** to experience different learning styles
- **Take breaks** if you need them - aikido is not about exhaustion
- **Ask questions** - our instructors love helping new students understand

## Injuries or Physical Limitations?

Please inform your instructor about any injuries, physical limitations, or health concerns. Aikido can often be adapted to work with your body's needs. We have practitioners with various backgrounds, ages, and physical abilities.
