## What is Aikido?

Aikido is a traditional Japanese martial art that emphasizes harmony and non-resistance. Rather than meeting force with force, Aikido teaches practitioners to blend with an attacker's energy and redirect it safely. This unique approach makes it suitable for people of all ages and fitness levels.

Kimori Dojo SVB is the Silicon Valley branch of Toshiharu Sawada's Ô-mei Kai Kimori Dojo, operating under Aikikai (the international aikido headquarters). We maintain the highest standards of authentic aikido training with instructors of 4th-8th dan rank.
